Seven specialised worlds

One platform. Built for different places.

Each Ruvanas pillar is being shaped for its own setting, while drawing on the same shared technology foundation.

01 / Retail

Ruvanas Retail

In-store radio for independent and multi-location brands, bringing music, promotions, announcements, scheduling and connected displays together.

02 / School

Ruvanas School

Safe school radio for staff and supervised students to record shows, prepare programmes and publish responsibly with Studio.

03 / Online Radio

Ruvanas Online Radio

Complete internet-station software with AutoDJ, live broadcasting, Studio production, players, podcasts and analytics.

04 / Health

Ruvanas Health

Controlled hospital and wellbeing audio, from calming programmes and announcements to moderated requests and supported displays.

05 / Faith

Ruvanas Faith

Broadcasting for churches and faith communities, connecting continuous radio, live services, sermons, teachings and podcasts.

06 / Organisations

Ruvanas Organisations

Media tools for NGOs, clubs and associations to create channels, events, announcements, podcasts and multi-branch communication.

07 / Inside

Ruvanas Inside

Secure media for correctional and institutional settings, with supervised production, governed programming, rehabilitation and controlled delivery. Not yet approved for production.

The creative workspace

Meet Ruvanas Studio.

From first recording to broadcast-ready programme, Studio is being developed as the production workspace within Ruvanas. It will bring waveform editing, multitrack production, programme preparation, manual playout and broadcast workflows together in one place.

Planned Basic and Pro experiences will support different levels of production work, with access shaped by the selected product and plan.
