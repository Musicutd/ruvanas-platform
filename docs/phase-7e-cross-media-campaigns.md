# Phase 7E — Cross-Media Campaigns

Stage 7E completes the core Stage 7 roadmap by coordinating approved promotional audio and digital signage under one Retail Media order.

## Delivered scope

- One commercial order can require audio, visual, or both media surfaces.
- Shared eligibility checks enforce the purchased inventory's locations, date range, and dayparts across both surfaces.
- Audio readiness requires an approved order creative, a published linked campaign, eligible target zones, and an eligible schedule.
- Visual readiness requires approved order assets, published linked playlists, eligible displays, eligible dates, and eligible daily windows.
- Overnight audio and visual windows are evaluated across both affected weekdays.
- Organisation owners and managers receive a final readiness view and may activate only a fully eligible order.
- Activation records the responsible user, time, immutable configuration hash, revision, and audit event.
- Combined delivery reports can be filtered to one Retail Media order while preserving separate audio and visual totals.

## Evidence boundary

Activation records that the configured delivery surfaces satisfy the approved order and inventory rules. It is not itself proof of delivery. Audio and visual proof remain separate device-confirmed technical events; neither is described as a listener, viewer, impression, reach, or commercial outcome.
