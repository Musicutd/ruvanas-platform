# Stage 18C — Interactive Admin Analytics and Alerts

## Outcome

The Ruvanas administration command centre now supports controlled 7, 14, 30 and 90-day reporting periods with real operational drill-downs.

## Included

- playback and newly started stream-session trend comparison;
- previous-period performance comparison;
- primary service-family distribution;
- top organisations and station-attributed playback;
- offline-player, health-incident, support and delivery-job action groups;
- protected, audit-recorded CSV management exports;
- permission-aware operational links;
- accessible chart descriptions and keyboard focus states;
- dedicated loading and recovery states for the administration overview.

## Safety and access

- all analytics are derived from aggregate or operational platform records;
- the management export requires a platform administrator session;
- export contents exclude passwords, tokens, IP addresses and protected payloads;
- export downloads are private, non-cacheable and recorded in the audit trail;
- Support users never receive Super Admin-only job controls.

## Verification

- controlled range and series aggregation tests;
- service-family classification tests;
- CSV escaping and content tests;
- static navigation, permission and accessibility assertions;
- full project test, static-integrity and production-build checks.
